# Post 01 — Reel script

**« Nesty est là »** · 35 seconds · 1080×1920 · French, subtitles burned in
Shot on a phone. Founder on camera. No motion graphics, no AI avatar, no stock.

---

## Before you press record

- Phone **vertical**, on anything stable at chest height, lens at **eye level**.
  A phone looking up at you is the single most amateur-looking mistake.
- **Face a window.** Daylight only. No ring light.
- Plain wall behind. Nothing on it. No other company's branding in frame.
- **Screen recordings first, on the real apps with the demo data.** Get them
  before you film yourself — you'll perform better once the hard part is done.
- Wear one solid colour. The brand is monochrome; the video should feel it.

## Screen recordings needed (record these first)

## Delivery notes

**The first 1.5 seconds decide everything.** "Nesty est là." lands before the
viewer has decided to scroll. Do not open with "Bonjour à tous", do not open with
a logo animation, do not open with "je m'appelle Achref". Your name goes nowhere
near the first ten seconds.

**Speak slightly faster than feels natural** and cut every pause in the edit. The
biggest gap between a founder video and a good founder video is dead air.

**Look at the lens, not the screen.** Put a small sticker next to the camera if
that helps.

**The 17.0 beat is the money line.** "Bloqué dans la base de données, pas dans un
rappel" is the sentence an agency owner repeats to a colleague. Say it slowly, let
it sit half a second, then cut.

**Do not read the Darija.** If a Tunisian phrase comes out naturally on a take —
« هاذي هي » before the last line — keep it, it's the most human second in the
video. If you have to translate it in your head first, drop it.

## Audio

Founder's voice is the track. If you add music, keep it at **8–12%** — a soft
minimal loop, nothing with a drop. If you use a trending sound for reach, put it
under the voice at 15% max and never let it take a beat on its own.

## Edit

CapCut on the phone is entirely sufficient. Straight cuts only — no zoom
transitions, no whip pans, no glitch effects. The look is calm and clean because
the brand is.

**Subtitles:** burned in, French, bottom third, Plus Jakarta Sans 600 or the
closest available, white on a black rounded pill. Do **not** use CapCut's animated
word-by-word preset — it makes a software company look like a dropshipper.

**Export:** 1080×1920, 30fps, MP4.
Save as `2026-XX-XX_post-01_instagram_reel.mp4` next to this file.

## Reuse

- Post to Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and TikTok — same file.
- LinkedIn: upload the **same video natively** (never a link to Instagram).
- Clip **20.0–28.0** on its own, with the caption « 8 photos. Une visite 360°. »,
