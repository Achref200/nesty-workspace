# Post 01 — « Nesty est là. »

**Goes live: Day 1, 18:30 Tunis time.**
Format: 5-slide carousel + 35-second founder reel.

---

## The job

Kill the "coming soon" impression. One post that makes Nesty a real, dated,
verifiable thing — with the product on screen, not a logo on a gradient.

This post is **not** trying to sell. It is trying to make the next two posts
credible. If someone screenshots this and sends it to another agency owner with
"regarde ça", it worked.

## Audience

Primary: **agency owners and agents in Sousse, Hammamet, Tunis.** They are the
ones we need in the DMs by Day 9.
Secondary: seekers (renters/buyers). They give us the reach that puts the post
in front of the primary audience — Tunisian real estate is a small world and
agencies watch what circulates.

Written *to* the agency owner. The seeker can enjoy it over their shoulder.

## The hook

> **« Nesty est là. »**

Three words. Deliberately flat — no exclamation mark, no rocket. In a feed of
"🔥 GRANDE NOUVELLE 🔥" the calm statement is the pattern interrupt, and it reads
like a company that doesn't need to shout.

The reel opens on the founder's face saying it in the first second. No intro.

## The one CTA

**Go look at the site.** That's it. Nothing else is asked.

Link in the **first comment** on Facebook and Instagram (in-body links get
throttled). In the LinkedIn body is fine — LinkedIn doesn't punish it as hard, and
the audience there is checking legitimacy anyway.

## Why these five slides

| # | Slide | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nesty est là. | Stops the scroll. Dates the launch. |
| 2 | Un site / espace agence / application / **un seul calendrier** | The positioning in four lines: one platform, not a bundle of tools. |
| 3 | The three surfaces | Proof of scope. This is what makes it look like a company, not a side project. |
| 4 | 8 photos → 360° → 0 apps | The teaser for post 02. Plants the differentiator early so post 02 lands on prepared ground. |
| 5 | Allez voir. C'est en ligne. | The ask, and the URL, large. |

Slide 4 is the one that gets saved. It is placed deep enough that the swipe-through
rate rewards the algorithm, and shallow enough that people reach it.

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] **LinkedIn page no longer says "coming soon"** — tagline, About, website link,
      cover image updated. *This post contradicts the page until that is done.*
- [ ] `nesty-tn.vercel.app` opens fast on a phone, on 4G, off wifi
- [ ] Carousel exported: 5 × PNG, 1080×1350, in slide order
- [ ] Reel exported: 1080×1920, subtitles burned in, under 40s
- [ ] Link ready to paste as the first comment, the second the post goes live
- [ ] Two hours free after posting to answer comments

## KPI

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Reach | 300+ |
| Swipe-through to slide 5 | 25%+ |
| Profile / page visits | 30+ |
| Site sessions on Day 1–2 | 40+ |
| DMs | *not the goal — do not chase* |

If reach is under 100, the problem is distribution, not the creative. Fix that
before making post 02 prettier: post from the personal profile, share into 3–4
Tunisian real-estate and Sousse-area Facebook groups, and send it directly to
20 people who will engage in the first hour.

## After posting

Pin the post on the Facebook page and the LinkedIn company page. It becomes the
default "what is Nesty" answer for anyone who lands there cold in the next month.
