# Post 03 — Reel script

**« 10 agences. Pas onze. »** · 40 seconds · 1080×1920 · French, subtitles burned in
**Founder-led, direct to camera.** This is the closest thing to a sales call we can put in a feed.

---

## The intent

This reel is one person making an offer to another person. It should feel like
the founder talking across a desk — not a promo, not an ad read.

The longest of the three (40s) because it carries a real offer with real terms.
Watch-time will drop and that is fine: the people still watching at second 35 are
the exact people we want, and they are the only ones the post is for.

## Delivery notes

**Second 26 to 33 is the reel.** « Je ne vous vends pas un logiciel fini » is a
disarming thing to say in an ad, which is exactly why it works. Slow down. Look
straight down the lens. Do not smile through it — say it like a fact you have
made peace with.

An agency owner has been pitched by people claiming to have everything solved.
Being the one who says "it's early, and that's the deal" is memorable and, more
importantly, it filters for the right partner.

**Say the price without flinching.** Do not soften it, do not rush past it, do
not add "seulement". In this market, hesitating on the number is read as knowing
it's too high. State it and stop talking.

**"Pas onze" needs to land dry.** It's a small joke and it works only if you
deliver it flat.

## What not to do

- ❌ Do not add a countdown timer or "PLUS QUE 3 PLACES" unless it is true. If
  three are genuinely taken by then, say it — that's the strongest version. If
  not, don't invent it. Everything else in this sprint is honest; one fake
  scarcity line contaminates all of it.
- ❌ Do not list features faster to fit more in. Three shown beats eight named.
- ❌ Do not end with "n'hésitez pas à nous contacter". End with the exact word
  you want typed.

## Audio

Voice only, or music under 10%. This reel should not sound produced. A slightly
imperfect room tone makes the offer feel more like a person and less like an ad.

## Edit

Straight cuts. Keep the founder's takes tight — cut every "euh", every restart,
every breath longer than half a second. Burned-in FR subtitles throughout.

Export 1080×1920, 30fps, MP4.
Save as `2026-XX-XX_post-03_instagram_reel.mp4` next to this file.

## Reuse — this is the important part

**Send this reel directly on WhatsApp to all 10 agencies on the outreach list**,
with a personalised line. See `captions.md` for the message.

A 40-second video of the founder making a clear offer, arriving in an agency
owner's WhatsApp, will outperform the public post by an order of magnitude with
this audience. The feed post is the credibility layer that makes the WhatsApp
message land — it is not the channel that closes.
