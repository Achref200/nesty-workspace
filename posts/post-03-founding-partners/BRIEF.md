# Post 03 — « 10 agences. Pas onze. »

**Goes live: Day 7, 18:30 Tunis time.**
Format: 5-slide carousel + 40-second founder reel.

---

## The job

**This is the post that has to produce DMs.** Posts 01 and 02 exist to make this
one credible. Everything in the nine days is judged on what this post generates.

Objective: **3+ qualified conversations with agency owners.** Not likes. Not
followers. Conversations we can turn into the first paying pilot.

## Why the ask comes on Day 7 and not Day 1

Because an agency owner will not respond to an offer from a company they've never
heard of. By Day 7 they've seen:

1. A product that visibly exists (post 01)
2. A capability their current setup doesn't have (post 02)

Now the offer reads as an opportunity rather than a cold pitch. Asking on Day 1
would have burned the whole sequence.

## The hook

> **« 10 agences. Pas onze. »**

The scarcity is **real** — the Founding Partner Program is genuinely capped at 10,
and that cap exists for a real reason: weekly 30-minute calls with each partner
don't scale past ten. That matters. Fake scarcity is transparent in a market this
small and it would cost more than it earns. This one survives being questioned,
which is the only kind worth using.

"Pas onze" is the line that makes it feel like a decision rather than a marketing
device.

## Price on slide 2, deliberately

Per the market context: **Tunisian agency owners ask "how much?" in the first
minute.** Dodging it with "contactez-nous pour un devis" reads as expensive and
evasive, and it's the fastest way to lose a lead here.

So: **99 TND/month, third slide of the carousel, in 190px type.** Confidently, with
the 199 that follows stated in the same breath — hiding the real price to make the
intro price look better is exactly the kind of thing this segment notices and
holds against you.

Negotiation is expected and cultural. The published number is the anchor; there is
room to move in the DM, which is where that conversation belongs.

## Slide 4 is the most important slide in the sprint

> « On ne vous vend pas un logiciel fini. »

This is the honest positioning, and honesty is the competitive advantage of a
pre-revenue company. We cannot beat an established vendor on maturity, references
or support hours. We can beat them on **access** — the founder answers the phone,
and their feedback shapes what gets built next quarter.

The two-column layout — *vous recevez* / *on vous demande* — frames it as an
exchange between equals, not a discount. An agency owner who signs up under this
framing arrives as a partner, tolerates rough edges, and gives us the feedback
that makes the product good. One who signs up thinking they bought finished
software churns in month two.

It also quietly does the work of setting expectations. Better here than in a
support conversation in six weeks.

## The one CTA

**Send the word « FONDATEUR ».** In a DM, in a comment, on WhatsApp.

One word, no form, no signup, no calendar link. Every additional step costs
roughly half the responses, and this segment converts through conversation, not
funnels.

**WhatsApp is the primary channel** — put the number in the first comment and in
the bio. It outperforms every other route with this audience.

## Why these five slides

| # | Slide | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **10** + ten numbered seats | Scarcity, made visual. The empty seats are the point. |
| 2 | **99 TND** | Answers the first question before it's asked. |
| 3 | What you actually get | Turns a price into value. Four concrete capabilities, no adjectives. |
| 4 | The exchange | Honesty as positioning. Sets expectations. |
| 5 | « FONDATEUR » | One word, one action. |

## The move most people miss

**Do not just post this and wait.** Send it directly, one by one, to the 10
identified agencies on WhatsApp, with a personal line:

> « Bonjour [Nom], je viens de publier ça. On ouvre 10 places, et j'ai pensé à
> [Agence]. Vous voulez que je vous montre l'outil en 15 minutes cette semaine ? »

The post makes the direct message credible — they can check the page and see a real
company. The post is the *proof*; the DM is the *channel*. A public post alone will
not close a Tunisian agency, and it was never supposed to.
