### ⚠️ The timezone trap — read this before editing any scheduled post

**This machine's browser reports `Europe/Paris` (UTC+2). Tunisia is UTC+1.**
LinkedIn schedules in the browser's timezone, and its dialog labels everything
"Central European Summer Time".

So **every time in the LinkedIn scheduler must be set one hour LATER than the
Tunis time you actually want.** 18:30 Tunis → enter **7:30 PM**.

If you reschedule from a phone or a machine set to Tunis time, that offset
disappears and the same "7:30 PM" would fire an hour late. Always re-check
against the "based on your location" line at the top of the dialog.

To edit or cancel: composer → clock icon → **View all scheduled posts** → `…`

## Why the sprint starts Friday 28 and not tomorrow

Two reasons, both practical:

1. **The assets don't exist yet.** The carousels are designed and ready to export,
   but the three reels have to be filmed, and the LinkedIn page still says
   "coming soon". Posting the announcement into a page that contradicts it wastes
   the one launch moment we get.
2. **The dates land better.** Starting Friday 28 puts all three posts on
   **Friday / Monday / Thursday** — strong days — while keeping the exact 3-day
   spacing. Starting tomorrow would put post 02 on a Sunday, the worst slot of
   the week for this audience.

Two prep days, then nine days of posting.

actually visible. Move the date, keep the pairing.
