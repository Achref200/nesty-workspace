# Post 02 — « Huit photos suffisent. »

**Goes live: Day 4, 18:30 Tunis time.**
Format: 5-slide carousel + 28-second screen-recording reel.

---

## The job

Post 01 said *we exist*. This one says *and we have something you cannot get
anywhere else*.

This is the **shareable** post of the three. Post 01 is an announcement — people
read announcements and move on. A 360° tour generated from eight ordinary phone
photos is a thing people **send to someone else**. Every share here is free reach
into exactly the network we can't buy our way into.

If one post in this sprint gets saved and forwarded in a WhatsApp group of agents,
it is this one.

## Why the 3D tour and not something else

We have several real differentiators. The calendar and the verification pipeline
are arguably more valuable to an agency day-to-day. But:

- **The calendar is boring to watch.** "Two bookings can't overlap" is a great
  sentence in a sales call and a terrible six seconds of video.
- **Verification is invisible.** It happens before anything appears.
- **The 3D tour is visual, instant, and unfamiliar.** You can *see* it work in
  four seconds with no explanation.

Social rewards what is legible without narration. That's the tour.

The calendar gets its moment in post 03, where the audience is already warm and
willing to read.

## The hook

> **« 8 photos suffisent. »**

The number does the work — a giant `8` filling a black frame. It creates the
question ("eight photos for what?") that the swipe answers. Curiosity gap, built
from a true fact.

## The one CTA

**« Envoyez-nous huit photos d'un de vos biens — on vous renvoie la visite 3D,
gratuitement. »**

This is the sharpest CTA in the whole sprint and it deserves defending: it costs
an agency almost nothing (eight photos they already have on their phone), it
produces something they can *keep and use*, and it puts us in a one-to-one
conversation with a warm lead. It is not a demo request — it is a free thing that
happens to require them to talk to us.

Reply keyword: **« 3D »** in comments or DM. Low friction, and every comment
feeds the algorithm.

> **Capacity check before posting:** this offer means manually producing tours
> for anyone who asks. Ten replies is a good day of work. If more than ~15 come
> in, answer everyone with a real timeline rather than going quiet — a slow yes
> keeps the lead, silence kills it.

## Why these five slides

| # | Slide | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Giant **8** | Curiosity gap. Unreadable-as-an-ad, which is why it stops the scroll. |
| 2 | 8 photos → 360° | Answers the question immediately. This is the "oh, I get it" slide. |
| 3 | Pas de casque / caméra 360 / plugin / appli | Kills every objection before it forms. Agencies assume 3D means expensive hardware — this is the slide that removes the assumption. |
| 4 | « Un client qui a déjà fait le tour… » | Translates the feature into **their** money and **their** Tuesday afternoon. Features don't sell; wasted trips across Sousse do. |
| 5 | Send us eight photos | The offer. |

Slide 4 is the one an agency owner nods at. Slide 3 is the one that removes the
"c'est pas pour nous" reflex.

## Production note — do this if you have 20 minutes

Slide 2 ships with eight numbered grey placeholders and it works. But if you
swap in **eight real photos of one real room**, the slide stops being a diagram
and becomes proof. The HTML has the one-line instruction inline.

Use a genuinely ordinary room shot on a phone. A too-perfect professional set
makes people assume the input requirement is higher than it is — which defeats
the entire point of the post.

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] Screen recording of a **real** tour, dragged around smoothly with a thumb
- [ ] The tour used is on a listing you have permission to show
- [ ] Slide 2 placeholders swapped for real photos (recommended, not required)
- [ ] Carousel exported: 5 × PNG, 1080×1350
- [ ] Reel exported: 1080×1920, subtitles burned in
- [ ] You have the bandwidth to actually deliver free tours to everyone who asks
- [ ] First comment ready with the link and the "3D" instruction

## KPI

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **Saves + shares** | **15+** ← the real one |
| Reach | 500+ (should beat post 01; the format travels) |
| Comments containing "3D" | 5+ |
| DMs asking for a free tour | 3+ |

**Saves and shares matter more than reach here.** A save is someone thinking "I
need to show this to my boss." That is the exact behaviour we want, and it is the
strongest algorithmic signal on Instagram.

## If it underperforms

The most likely failure is that it reads as a *tech demo* rather than a *business
argument*. If reach is fine but saves are low, re-cut the reel to open on slide
4's line — « ne traverse pas Sousse pour découvrir que la chambre est trop
petite » — and put the technology second. Lead with the wasted afternoon, not the
360°.
