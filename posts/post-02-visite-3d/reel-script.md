# Post 02 — Reel script

**« 8 photos → une visite 360° »** · 28 seconds · 1080×1920 · French, subtitles burned in
**Screen-recording led.** The founder appears twice, briefly. The product is the star.

---

## The one rule for this reel

**The viewer must see the tour move before second 8.** Everything else is
negotiable. If your edit delays the 360° drag past 8 seconds, cut whatever is in
front of it — including your own face.

This reel is not explaining a feature. It is showing a small magic trick and then
saying what it's worth.

## Delivery notes

**The 4.5s black cut is deliberate.** A half-second of nothing before the reveal
is what makes the reveal feel like a reveal. Do not fill it with a transition
effect.

**Second 6 to 14 is the whole reel.** Eight seconds of a tour smoothly moving
under a thumb. Resist the urge to cut it shorter — this is the part people watch
twice, and watch-time is what pushes the reel out to new viewers.

**Say the last line like you've lived it.** « Traverser Sousse pour découvrir que
la chambre est trop petite » should sound like something that has actually
annoyed you, not a line you're reading. Every agent watching has done that drive.

**Do not say "intelligence artificielle", "innovation", or "révolutionnaire".**
The trick speaks for itself, and those words make a viewer suspicious rather than
impressed.

## Filming Clip C properly

This is worth ten minutes of care:

- Use a **real listing** with a **real tour** — one you have permission to show.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb. A notification banner mid-drag ruins the take.
- Clean status bar: full battery, no wifi icon drama, no 47 unread badges.
- Drag with **one thumb, slowly, in one continuous arc**. Left → right, then a
  small look up, then settle. No flicking.
- Record in portrait, full screen, no browser chrome if you can avoid it.

## Audio

Voice first. Music optional at 8–12%. **Nothing on the 4.5s black cut** — silence
is the effect.

If you use a trending sound for reach, drop it to 15% and never let it play alone.

## Edit

Straight cuts. Burned-in FR subtitles, white on black pill, bottom third.
Export 1080×1920, 30fps, MP4.

Save as `2026-XX-XX_post-02_instagram_reel.mp4` next to this file.

## Reuse

- **Clip C alone, 8 seconds, looping**, captioned « Générée à partir de 8 photos »
  is a strong standalone Story and a good ad creative if you ever run paid.
- Send the same 8-second clip **directly in WhatsApp** to the 10 agencies on the
  outreach list. It is the single most persuasive 8 seconds we have, and in this
  market a WhatsApp message outperforms any feed post.
- Keep the raw tour link — sending a prospect a live tour they can drag themselves
  beats any video of one.
